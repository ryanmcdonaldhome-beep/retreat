The shape of it

Arrive and play. One working morning. Then nothing.

This is the skeleton every option follows. The cards above change the where; this is the what. It is last year's shape with the two things people asked for added: a hot seat for everyone and more of the afternoon free.

Day one, WednesdayArrive and settle
  • Roll in from 2 pm. Welcome bag in your room.
  • Free afternoon. Optional walk, spa or nap.
  • 7:30 pm fire pit or lounge. Drinks on the group.
  • The photo game starts the moment you arrive.
Day two, ThursdayThe working morning
  • 8:30 breakfast together.
  • 9:00 first block. 9:45 second block. Topics are open; suggest one.
  • 10:30 coffee.
  • 10:45 hot seats: seven minutes each, one problem, the room solves it.
  • 12:00 lunch.
  • 1:00 "Steal this": everyone shows one thing that worked in 2026 in four minutes.
  • 2:00 done. Hot tub, spa, ski, nothing.
  • 6:30 the big dinner. Photo game awards at dessert.
Day three, FridayGo home, or don't
  • Breakfast on your own.
  • Check out by 11. Stay on for the spa if you like.
  • Flyers: afternoon flight home.

What to bring

  • One problem you want the room to solve in seven minutes.
  • One thing that worked in 2026: a script, a tool, a habit. You get four minutes to show it.
  • Swimsuit. There is always a hot tub.
  • Your phone, charged. The photo game needs it.
  • Something warm for the fire. Boots if we are in the mountains.
  • A number you are chasing in 2027. We write them down and read them back next year.

How the money works

  • Last year everyone paid Geneva one invoice of about $400 for the working morning, meals and the dinner, and booked their own room with a group code. Same plan this year.
  • The brokerage card holds the hotel contract. Ryan is the on-site contact.
  • If we fly, everyone books the same flights. The site will list the flight numbers once dates are set.
  • Drinks at dinner are on you. Drinks at the fire are on the group.
